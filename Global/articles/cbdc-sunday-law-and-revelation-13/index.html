Digital Currency, Sunday Law & Revelation 13 — A Biblical Perspective

As programmable finance and global legislative trends converge, Revelation 13 calls believers to sober discernment, faithfulness to God’s commandments, and calm trust in Christ.

Discussions around Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) and programmable payment controls have prompted many Christians to revisit the prophetic warnings of Revelation 13 regarding economic coercion and worship.

Scripture never calls believers to panic or sensationalism. Instead, Revelation 14:12 points to patient endurance: "Here is the patience of the saints; here are those who keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus."

True preparedness begins in the heart today—anchoring daily life in God’s Word, integrity, and prayerful watchfulness.
