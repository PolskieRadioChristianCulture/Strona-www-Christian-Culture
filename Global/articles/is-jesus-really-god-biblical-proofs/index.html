Is Jesus Really God? Clear Biblical Evidence

From Old Testament messianic prophecies to the explicit testimony of the Gospels and Apostles, Scripture consistently proclaims the divine identity of Jesus Christ.

Did Jesus merely claim to be a moral teacher, or did He reveal Himself as the eternal Son of God? Throughout the New Testament, Jesus forgives sins, accepts worship, declares "Before Abraham was, I AM" (John 8:58), and exercises sovereign authority over creation.

The Apostle John opens his Gospel with unambiguous clarity: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God" (John 1:1). Paul writes that in Christ "all the fullness of the Deity lives in bodily form" (Colossians 2:9).

Understanding Christ’s true identity is not an abstract theological debate—it is the foundation of salvation, hope, and personal fellowship with the living God.
