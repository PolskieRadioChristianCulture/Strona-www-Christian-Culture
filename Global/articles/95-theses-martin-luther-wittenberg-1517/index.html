Martin Luther’s 95 Theses (Wittenberg 1517) — Return to Scripture Alone

On October 31, 1517, a single call for academic debate sparked a global spiritual awakening centered on Sola Scriptura, Sola Fide, and Sola Gratia.

When Martin Luther challenged the sale of indulgences in Wittenberg, his deepest concern was pastoral and biblical: salvation cannot be bought with silver, nor earned by human merit, but is received through living faith in Jesus Christ.

The Reformation called the Church back to the supreme authority of the Bible (Sola Scriptura) and placed the Word of God directly into the hands and language of ordinary people.

Five centuries later, that same invitation stands: return to the living Word of God, free from human traditions that obscure the grace of Christ.
