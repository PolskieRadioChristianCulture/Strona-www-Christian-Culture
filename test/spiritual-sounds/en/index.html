Mission

Music that leads you closer to God

Spiritual Sounds is a space where faith meets music. The channel features Psalms in new musical arrangements, Christian songs, prayerful reflections and musical interpretations of Scripture.

Its purpose is to create music filled with peace, hope and a biblical message for people seeking prayer, reflection, worship and spiritual encouragement.

Psalms & Scripture

The Book of Psalms and Scripture in music

The main Spiritual Sounds collection contains hundreds of musical interpretations of the Book of Psalms and other passages of Scripture in many different styles – from peaceful, reflective arrangements to powerful vocal and instrumental compositions.

The playlist continues to grow as new recordings are added by its creator.
