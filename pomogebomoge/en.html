Before you give

Does this page confirm my gift?

No. Copying a number or clicking a button is not a payment. Payment happens only in your bank or on Patronite, Revolut or Zrzutka.pl, where you also see its confirmation.

Can I deduct my gift from my taxes?

We do not promise that. Transfers go to a private person — Cezary Rogowski. Please ask your tax adviser about your situation.

I live abroad. How can I help?

BLIK phone transfers work only with Polish banks, and our accounts are in PLN. From abroad, Revolut or patronage on Patronite is the simplest way.

What is the difference between patronage and a gift?

Patronage on Patronite is regular monthly support that you can end at any time. A bank transfer, BLIK, Revolut or a fundraiser is a one-off gift.

Where does the money go?

The “Rozwój Christian Culture” account funds the studio, radio, television and technology. The “Dar misyjny” account funds apps, Bible content and evangelism. Patronage and online gifts support the whole mission.

Do you publish donors' names?

Only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw — and never with an amount or bank details.
