The Mission

More Than Music

If even one person finds the courage to turn to Jesus Christ through a song, Ark of Fire has fulfilled its mission.

Testimony and origins

Ark of Fire is an independent Christian rock and metal project from the Netherlands, founded in a personal love of music and faith in Jesus Christ.

Its founder wishes to remain anonymous. The purpose of the project is not personal fame, but to bring the message of hope, grace and redemption to those who may need it most.

The sound moves between heavy metal, rock, blues and quieter passages. Its message remains centered on Jesus Christ.

The project’s founder describes experiences of love, loss, grief and doubt, and a faith sustained by the conviction that God’s grace is greater than our darkest moments.

  • HopeSongs for those walking through loss, grief and doubt.
  • GraceA message that God’s grace is greater than our darkest moments.
  • RedemptionJesus Christ at the center of every story.
The Human and the Machine

Meet Layla Farzal

Layla Farzal is a fictional digital vocal identity and artistic alter ego created with AI technology. She is not a real-life singer. Human direction shapes the stories, lyrics, arrangements and creative vision; AI helps realize the music and voice.

Transparency: AI-assisted music project. The founder is intentionally anonymous. No real-person identity is claimed for the digital singer.

Contest Rules & Privacy Notice

1. General Information

  • Contest Name: Faith Testimony Contest “Keep the Fire of Faith Alive!” (Ark of Fire × Christian Culture Global).
  • Technical Organizer: Christian Culture — independent Christian missionary media initiative (responsible for the entry form, submission handling, and winner outreach).
  • Partner & Prize Sponsor: Ark of Fire (independent Christian rock & metal project from the Netherlands).
  • Duration: November 13, 2026 through November 30, 2026 (23:59 CET).
  • No Purchase Necessary: participation is completely free of charge and does not require any purchase or donation.

2. Eligibility & Winner Selection

  1. To participate, complete the form at https://polskieradio.cc/ArkOfFire/en/ (or the Polish version), provide your name or nickname, a valid email address, answer the contest question: “How do you keep the fire of faith alive in your everyday life?” (40–2000 characters), and accept these rules and the privacy notice.
  2. Only one entry per person is permitted.
  3. The organizer reserves the right to disqualify offensive, spam, unlawful, or completely off-topic submissions.
  4. Selection Criteria: From all eligible entries, Ark of Fire will select one winner based on the content, sincerity, personal testimony, relevance to everyday Christian life, and Christ-centered encouragement. No chance-based or automated selection method is used.

3. Prize & Shipping

  • Prize: one official Ark of Fire T-shirt (men’s M or women’s F model, winner’s chosen size).
  • Shipping Costs: both the prize and international shipping costs are fully covered by Ark of Fire.
  • Winner Notification: Christian Culture will contact the selected winner by email by December 3, 2026. The winner has 7 calendar days to respond. If the winner does not respond or declines, Ark of Fire may select another winner from the remaining entries.
  • The winner’s full name will not be published without separate explicit consent.

4. Privacy Notice

  • The data controller for administering this contest is Christian Culture (contact: polskiercctv@gmail.com).
  • At the entry stage, we collect only your name or nickname, email address, and contest answer.
  • For judging purposes, Ark of Fire receives only the entry ID, name/nickname, language, and testimony text — email addresses are never shared with Ark of Fire at this stage.
  • Shipping details (full name, shipping address, T-shirt size, and M/F model) are collected solely from the winner after selection and shared privately with Ark of Fire only upon explicit consent: “I consent to Christian Culture sharing my full name, shipping address, T-shirt size and selected model with Ark of Fire solely for the purpose of delivering the prize.”
  • Personal data is retained only for as long as needed to run the contest and deliver the prize, and is deleted afterwards. You may request access, correction, or erasure of your data at any time.
