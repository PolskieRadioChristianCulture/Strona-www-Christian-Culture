CC HELP · PARENTING — A SHARED EVERYDAY PLAN

Share responsibilities. Make room for connection.

A practical starting point for parents and carers: one concern, clear tasks and attention to a child’s needs.

Start with one step ↗Where to find help →

Respect for your situation. No judgement and no promises of instant solutions.

If you need help now

For an immediate threat to life or health call 112. Do not wait for Christian Culture to reply.

Children and young people in Poland: 800 12 12 12 — free, around the clock. Ask about language availability. RPD support chat.

Sources and service details below ↓

Three first steps

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one manageable step.

  1. 01

    Choose one difficult moment

    A morning, after-school period or evening: name the situation you want to improve. Avoid changing every rule at once.

  2. 02

    Share specific tasks

    Agree who does what and how to request cover. Match a child’s contribution to their abilities; adults remain responsible for safety.

  3. 03

    Arrange a short review

    After a few days, ask what helped and what needs adjustment. A plan can change without shaming someone when something did not work.

PREPARE FOR A CONVERSATION

Predictability rather than constant control

Explain rules and leave room for questions and age-appropriate choices. Agree on device use and privacy together. Do not publish sensitive stories about a child to win other adults’ support.

Support for the carer

Families have different resources and opportunities to share work. Ask trusted people or suitable services for concrete help. Persistent overload is worth discussing with a professional without blaming yourself or the child.

Good to know

Should this plan fit every family?

No. It prepares a conversation, not a universal daily schedule. Consider age, needs, health and each household member’s circumstances.

Do we have to describe our home to CC?

No. You can write a plan privately on paper. This page does not require a child’s details, address or family relationship information.
